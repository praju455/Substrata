The problem

Your suppliers look independent. Several of them quietly draw from the same source, two tiers down.

One event. Six steps. Weeks of warning.

Substrata infers which suppliers secretly share a source, measures how long before production is hit, and proposes governed fixes. A person approves anything high-stakes.
